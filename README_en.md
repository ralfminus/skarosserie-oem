## 🎯 Strategic Focus: Bridging the Gap between Production Tooling & Manual Craft

For special vehicle editions, prototypes, and low-volume production (1 to 500 units), multi-million-euro steel stamping dies are economically unviable and inflexible. Pure manual fabrication, on the other hand, quickly hits limitations regarding OEM tolerances, panel stiffness, and takt times.

**SKAROSSERIE delivers the industrial symbiosis:**
1. **Agile Digital Process Chain:** GOM ATOS 3D optical metrology (< 0.02 mm accuracy per VDI/VDE 2634), ZEISS GOM Digital Twin / CAD reverse engineering, 5-axis CNC rapid tooling from Ureol and composite materials in days or weeks instead of months.
2. **Large-Scale Stretch Forming:** Dedicated industrial stretch-forming press for roofs, bonnets, quarter panels, and closures. Requiring only a single male punch—delivering full-surface strain hardening for +35% higher dent resistance and flawless Class-A surfaces.
3. **Master Coachbuilding Craftsmanship:** Eckold Kraftformer, pneumatic hammer shaping in clamping frames, and precision dressing on forming bucks for airtight shut lines and crisp feature lines.
4. **OEM Joining Technologies:** KUKA 6-axis robotic laser welding (minimal heat-affected zone), Böllhoff self-piercing riveting for multi-material hybrid joints (aluminum/steel), and zero-distortion clinching.
